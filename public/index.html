Onetangi Valley

The Pinnacle of Waiheke Syrah, Overlooking Onetangi Valley 

Waiheke has many unique sites across the Island. All meticously cared for over the 30 years of Waiheke Viticulture. 

Kingi wines works with closely with other island vineyards and wineries. In 2026,  an opportunity came about to source fruit from one of the islands most renowned  Syrah blocks. 

A north aspect over looking the Onetangi Valley, this block planted to MS clone Syrah produces some truly unique fruit. Vines were established over 30 years ago, cared for by hand all along the way. 

Church Bay

Organic mindset, a vineyard that exudes viticultural passion.

When thinking of viticultural consistency on Waiheke, Jurassic Ridge Vineyard is the first that comes to mind. An organic approach that requires intensive labour, the result; an extremely healthy vineyard with premium quality fruit. 

Sitting above Church Bay in a protected position, the soils consist of thriving top soil on heavy clay. Just as areas of the Chinon appellation in the Loire Valley, these soils produce elegant Cabernet Franc, balanced and complex. 

Both sites are picked by hand and vinified separately.
How the wine is made
Winemaking

Naturally fermented, and left alone.

Having learnt under some of New Zealand's best winemakers, Josh Kingston “Kingi” makes wines with a minimalist intervention mindset.

Having gained international experience through multiple vintages in the Rhone Valley and Sonoma Coast, with ultra premium producers. Josh knows the importance of pure fruit expression. 

Waiheke vineyards produce some of New Zealand's finest wines. A unique sense of place in every bottle. Allowing the fruit to truly represent a moment in time. 



01
Hand-picked
Harvested by hand into small crates. Meticulously sorted for the best quality. 
02
Indigenous natural yeast
Wines are fermented naturally using the yeast already present on the grapes and ambient in the winery.
03
Gentle management
The reds cap is hand plunged twice daily through fermentation.
04
Traditionally bottled
Small addition of sulphur. Fined traditionally and selectively with egg white.
